Early Recognition of Parkinson’s Disease using Vocal Bio-Marks

This project explores the use of vocal bio-markers for the early recognition of Parkinson’s Disease. Parkinson’s often manifests subtle changes in speech patterns long before motor symptoms become evident. By analyzing vocal signals with machine learning models, the system aims to detect early indicators of the disease, enabling timely diagnosis and intervention. The research integrates signal processing, feature extraction, and classification algorithms to distinguish between healthy and Parkinson’s-affected speech samples. This approach demonstrates the potential of AI-driven healthcare solutions to improve patient outcomes through non-invasive, accessible diagnostic tools.
